# Marisol Bay — an open-world crime game

[![Rank](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/TonyWanders/make-gta6/rank.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)
[![Reward](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/TonyWanders/make-gta6/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)

Marisol Bay is an original open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built for the [Make GTA 6 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01) on make.fun. Explore the sun-soaked coastal city of Marisol Bay on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the Marisol Bay Police, take on six story missions for fixer Rosa Vega, and build your reputation from street driver to Bay Legend.

Play it: serve `game/` with any static server (`node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080) and play in desktop Chrome. No network requests, no build step, all art and sound made in code.

Controls: WASD move and drive, E enter or exit a car, mouse aim, click to shoot, Shift sprint, M mission list, P pause, Enter restart after busted or wasted. Progress saves automatically.
